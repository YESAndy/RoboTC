# Third-party software

RoboTC uses these open-source libraries. Their license texts are distributed with their packages in `node_modules` after installation.

- Three.js — MIT — https://github.com/mrdoob/three (including OrbitControls).
- fflate — MIT — https://github.com/101arrowz/fflate.
- Vite — MIT — https://github.com/vitejs/vite (build tooling).
- TypeScript — Apache-2.0 — https://github.com/microsoft/TypeScript (build tooling).
- tsx — MIT — https://github.com/privatenumber/tsx (test tooling).

The office and robot are generated from code. No third-party models, photos, remote fonts, or textures are loaded by the application.
